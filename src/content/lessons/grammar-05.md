Here's a trick question for Spanish speakers: Would it be better in Portuguese to say the equivalent of <i lang="es">Siéntate</i> or <i lang="es">Siéntese</i>? Answer: Don't worry about the reflexive pronouns. Chances are that Brazilians won't use them either. In this lesson Valdo and Michelle help the rest of us to get a sense of the disappearing reflexive pronouns in Portuguese. Michelle also adds how cool she thinks the use of coupons is here in Texas as well.
