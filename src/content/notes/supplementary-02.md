## Chart #1: Major consonant sounds in Portuguese

| Symbol | Examples | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| p | <i lang="pt-BR"><mark>p</mark>ata, ca<mark>p</mark>a</i> | Similar to English without aspiration |
| b | <i lang="pt-BR"><mark>b</mark>ala, <mark>b</mark>o<mark>b</mark>o</i> | Similar to English |
| t | <i lang="pt-BR"><mark>t</mark>apa, <mark>t</mark>a<mark>t</mark>u, a<mark>t</mark>é</i> | Similar to English without aspiration, may be dental or alveolar |
| d | <i lang="pt-BR"><mark>d</mark>ata, <mark>d</mark>a<mark>d</mark>o</i> | Similar to English, may be dental or alveolar |
| k | <i lang="pt-BR"><mark>c</mark>apa, va<mark>c</mark>a, chi<mark>qu</mark>e</i> | Similar to English without aspiration |
| g | <i lang="pt-BR"><mark>g</mark>ata, á<mark>g</mark>ua</i> | Similar to English |
| tʃ | <i lang="pt-BR"><mark>t</mark>ia, ten<mark>t</mark>e</i> | Palatalization of <span lang="pt-BR-fonipa">/t/</span> typical of the southeast of Brazil |
| dʒ | <i lang="pt-BR"><mark>d</mark>ia, des<mark>d</mark>e</i> | Palatalization of <span lang="pt-BR-fonipa">/d/</span> typical of the southeast of Brazil |
| f | <i lang="pt-BR"><mark>f</mark>ica, es<mark>f</mark>orço</i> | Similar to English |
| v | <i lang="pt-BR"><mark>v</mark>aca, a<mark>v</mark>ô</i> | Similar to English |
| s | <i lang="pt-BR"><mark>s</mark>ala, ma<mark>ss</mark>a, ca<mark>ç</mark>a, e<mark>s</mark>tá, rapa<mark>z</mark></i> | Similar to English |
| z | <i lang="pt-BR"><mark>z</mark>ero, ca<mark>s</mark>a, Li<mark>s</mark>boa</i> | Similar to English |
| ʃ | <i lang="pt-BR"><mark>ch</mark>á, a<mark>ch</mark>a, e<mark>s</mark>tá, rapa<mark>z</mark></i> | "ch" everywhere, syllable-final "s" is a dialectal variation |
| ʒ | <i lang="pt-BR"><mark>j</mark>á, se<mark>j</mark>a, <mark>g</mark>eral, <mark>g</mark>ibi, Li<mark>s</mark>boa</i> | "j, gi, ge" everywhere, syllable-final "s" is a dialectal variation |
| x | <i lang="pt-BR"><mark>R</mark>oma, ca<mark>rr</mark>o, amo<mark>r</mark>, ca<mark>r</mark>ta</i> | Voiceless velar fricative |
| h | <i lang="pt-BR"><mark>R</mark>oma, ca<mark>rr</mark>o, amo<mark>r</mark>, ca<mark>r</mark>ta</i> | Voiceless glottal fricative, dialectal variation |
| ɻ | <i lang="pt-BR">amo<mark>r</mark>, ca<mark>r</mark>ta</i> | Voiced retroflex alveolar, dialectal variation |
| r | <i lang="pt-BR"><mark>R</mark>oma, ca<mark>rr</mark>o, amo<mark>r</mark>, ca<mark>r</mark>ta</i> | Alveolar trill |
| ɣ | <i lang="pt-BR">ca<mark>r</mark>ga, ama<mark>r</mark>ga</i> | Voiced velar fricative, dialectal variation |
| ɦ | <i lang="pt-BR">ca<mark>r</mark>ga, ama<mark>r</mark>ga</i> | Voiced glottal fricative, dialectal variation |
| ɾ | <i lang="pt-BR">pa<mark>r</mark>a, b<mark>r</mark>anca, amo<mark>r</mark>, ca<mark>r</mark>ta</i> | Alveolar tap |
| m | <i lang="pt-BR"><mark>m</mark>ala, a<mark>m</mark>a</i> | Similar to English |
| n | <i lang="pt-BR"><mark>n</mark>ada, A<mark>n</mark>a</i> | Similar to English |
| ɲ or j̃ | <i lang="pt-BR">se<mark>nh</mark>or, ba<mark>nh</mark>o</i> | Palatal nasal or nasalized palatal approximant |
| l | <i lang="pt-BR"><mark>l</mark>ata, ba<mark>l</mark>a</i> | Similar to English |
| ɫ or w | <i lang="pt-BR">sa<mark>l</mark>, ca<mark>l</mark>ma, Brasi<mark>l</mark></i> | Velarized lateral or labio-velarized approximant |
| ʎ or lʲ | <i lang="pt-BR">abe<mark>lh</mark>a, ve<mark>lh</mark>a</i> | Palatal lateral or palatalized <span lang="pt-BR-fonipa">/l/</span> |
